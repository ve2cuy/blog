# Project 2 – One tube receiver

*Document converti depuis [ve2cuy.com/blog](https://ve2cuy.com/blog/index.php/vacuum-tube/one-tube-receiver/) — dernière modification : 2010-05-23*

---

Here is a one tube AM radio with a RF section (6EA7 H-MU) and a AF section (6EA7 L-MU).  The AF section is able to drive a speaker.

For a one tube radio, we get decent reception.  Listen for yourself:

.   

▶️ Vidéo YouTube : [Voir la vidéo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eifBVVg4lSI)

In the video, H-MU section (RF) drain 0.21ma and L-MU (AF) drain 18.9 ma.

B+ is around 150 volt.
